Produce a batch

Before importing: check Zoho by hand for an existing Opportunity on each buyer. The Monitor never searches the CRM (D31) and the person importing is the only duplicate control. Every duplicate that reaches a batch is logged as a finding and counted at the week 14 gate.

Records already exported are never in a second batch. A range with nothing new in it still produces a batch, with a header row and no rows, so a run that found nothing is on the record.
